## Handout (mts/handout/)

Two-sided US Letter print handout + TV screens, built from `menu.json` via `build.py` and deployed to https://midtownbbq.io/handout/ (CloudFront distribution `E293E6RVQ0BLMD`).

- Source of truth: `menu.json` (copy/prices) and `build.py` (HTML generation). `handout.html` is regenerated by `build.py` — do not edit it directly; edits won't survive a rebuild.
- Rebuild: `cd mts/handout && python3 build.py` (generates `handout.html`, `screens.html`, and `exports/` PDF/PNG). Needs `google-chrome` for PDF export.
- Deploy: `cd mts/handout && ./deploy.sh` (uploads `handout.html`/`styles/menu.css`/`menu.json` to S3 bucket `midtownbbq-io` under `handout/`, then invalidates CloudFront `/handout/*`). Invalidation may take ~1–2 min to propagate.
- Front-page quirks to know:
  - Sections with an `image` in `menu.json` render as full-width photo panels (`.side-section`) on page 1.
  - Fixed photo heights live in `build.py`'s `page_css` (e.g. `#section-quesadilla`, `#section-crazyPotato`); Whole Wings hero is capped separately.
  - "Front"/"Back" labels are `.page-label` (yellow, Oswald, drop shadow).
  - Page-1 column balance is controlled by the `handoutPages[0].columns` arrays in `menu.json`.
