OUR STORY.

Southfield Roots.

Tradition in Every Bite.

Midtown Smokehouse wasn't born in a boardroom; it was born in a backyard. What started as a passion for the perfect bark and a deep respect for the slow-smoke process has grown into a community staple.

We carry the spirit of Southfield in everything we do, honoring the techniques that have defined real barbecue for generations.

The Art of the Pit.

  • 01 Hardwood Only: We use locally sourced oak to ensure a clean, deep smoke profile.
  • 02 Low & Slow: No shortcuts. Our meats spend 12+ hours in the pit, undisturbed.
  • 03 Hand-Rubbed: Every cut is seasoned by hand with our signature spice blend.

The Process.

Respect the Smoke.

True barbecue is a test of patience. We believe that if you rush the meat, you lose the soul. That's why we rely on the natural rhythm of the fire and the steady flow of smoke to do the heavy lifting.
